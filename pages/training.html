YOUR TRAINING PLAN

MASTER THE BASICS

Choose a focus and take it one rep at a time.

01

SHOOTING

FIND YOUR
RHYTHM.

A repeatable shot starts with balance and ends with a relaxed, consistent follow-through.

FOCUS ON

  • Set your feet and keep your balance through the release.
  • Line up your shooting elbow beneath the ball.
  • Finish with a soft wrist and hold your follow-through.
TRY THIS: Make 5 close-range form shots from five spots, then gradually step back. Track makes, not just attempts.

02

BALL HANDLING

CONTROL
THE PACE.

Keep the ball protected and your eyes up so you can read the floor, not just your dribble.

FOCUS ON

  • Use your fingertips and push the ball into the floor.
  • Stay low with your knees bent and your chest up.
  • Practice with both hands and keep your eyes forward.
TRY THIS: Alternate right- and left-hand stationary dribbles for 30 seconds each. Progress to controlled crossover and change-of-pace moves.
Watch a ball-handling lesson ↗
03

FOOTWORK & FITNESS

MOVE WITH
PURPOSE.

Build a strong base with balanced footwork, controlled changes of direction and smart conditioning.

FOCUS ON

  • Warm up dynamically before intense activity.
  • Stay balanced when stopping, landing and changing direction.
  • Keep good form and take recovery seriously.
TRY THIS: Mark two lines a few steps apart. Sprint, stop under control, and return at an easy pace. Repeat only while your movement stays clean.
